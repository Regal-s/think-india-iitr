Ram Mandir Pran Pratistha Screening

Debate

Chhatra Sansad 1.0

Parliamentary-style debate on "One Nation, One Election" — held on Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Jayanti. Judged by Prof. Akhilesh Misra, Shri Sumit Pandey, and Shri Sanjiv Jaineet. Participants took an oath upholding equality and justice.

Civic

Voter Awareness Campaign

Election Awareness Programme at LBS Ground, organized with SAC, Cultural Council, and NSS. Under the slogan "First Vote, Must Vote", 800+ students formed a human chain ahead of the 17th Lok Sabha elections. First 200 registrants received campaign T-shirts.

Apr 15, 2024·800+ students · LBS Ground
Lecture

DST Lecture — Research Funding

Session on Research Funding Opportunities by Dr. S.K. Varshney, Advisor & Former Head of International Cooperation at DST. Covered internship programs, travel grants, and doctoral funding. Held in hybrid format with 900+ registrations across disciplines.

May 21, 2024·900+ registrations · Hybrid
Yatra

Tiranga Yatra 2024

Independence Day march carrying two 500-metre national flags across campus. Initiated by Director Prof. K.K. Pant from the Multi-Activity Centre. Students, faculty, and staff marched chanting "Vande Mataram" and "Bharat Mata Ki Jai". Concluded with a national integrity pledge.

Aug 15, 2024·Across campus
Lecture

Reviving Bharat — A Scientific Civilisation

Seminar titled "Reviving Bharat: A Scientific Civilization and its Relevance in Present Times" by Mr. Aditya Jha, Senior Application Engineer at Oracle and IIT Roorkee alumnus. Explored India's spiritual and scientific heritage in the context of modern challenges.

Sep 18, 2024·APJ AKB-103, IITR
Debate

Chhatra Sansad 2.0

Debate on "Life With/Without AI: Maximizing Efficiency or Jeopardizing Human Autonomy?" Participants explored AI's $15.7 trillion projected economic contribution against risks of 300 million job displacements. Judged by Prof. Neha Rajput, Dr. Raghav Chawla, and Shri Ankit Suri.

Oct 23, 2024·IIT Roorkee
Flagship

National Convention 2024 — Bharat 4.0

Three days, 280+ student delegates from IITs, NITs, IIMs, IISc and NLUs — a working room for Bharat 4.0, the India of 2047. Themed sessions on infrastructure, cybersecurity, legal reform, media, and sustainability. 102 student volunteers. Ideathon judged by NITI Aayog and IIT faculty.

Dec 21–23, 2024·IIT Roorkee
Workshop

Mindset Makeover

A transformative 9-day online challenge blending ancient wisdom with modern strategies. Featured Mr. Aditya Jha on discipline and early rising; Aman Swaraj (PhD, IIT Roorkee) on lessons from the Mahabharata; and Mr. Avdhesh Sharma (Microsoft) on consistency. 110+ participants.

Jan 1–9, 2025·110+ participants · Online

National Youth Day — Vivekananda Lecture

Online lecture commemorating Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary, delivered by Swami Sarvalokananda. Topics included student life, leadership, ideal role models, and problem-solving inspired by Vivekananda's journey. 100+ students participated.

Cultural

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Jayanti

A celebration honouring the legacy of one of India's greatest warrior-kings — inaugurated by Prof. Bhavesh Kumar R. Bhalja with the lighting of a ceremonial diya. Vibrant Maharashtrian folk dance performances and a stirring recitation of Powada brought Shivaji's era to life.

Feb 21, 2025·IIT Roorkee
Workshop

NitiShala — Policy Negotiation Workshop

A two-hour policy negotiation workshop by Mr. Nikhil Arya, Community Manager at Rashtram School of Public Leadership — UN Karmaveer Chakra awardee (2021). Students role-played policymakers negotiating port infrastructure, navigating environmental compliance, labour laws, and national development.

Feb 22, 2025·IIT Roorkee

Corporate Compass

Debate on "Uniform Civil Code" — exploring Article 44 of the Constitution and its implications for national integration vs. cultural freedom. Judged by Shri Arun Kanwaji (Supreme Court Advocate). Best Speaker: Ms. Aditi Mehra (Humanities). Runner-Up: Mr. Saurabh Joshi (Civil Engg.).

Apr 23, 2025·IIT Roorkee
Workshop

Design Workshop

Two-hour online graphic and visual design workshop by Ar. Sehaj Singh, open to IIT Roorkee students and participants from colleges across India. Covered design principles, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Canva, and the role of AI in modern design. 60 participants.

Apr 27, 2025·60 participants · Online
Lecture

Master Your Mind

An evening of meditation, Sanatan philosophy, and inner transformation at the MAC Auditorium. Speaker: Mr. Avedhesh Sharma — IITR alumnus and Software Development Engineer at Microsoft — sharing how ancient Indian wisdom applies to modern student life. 650+ participants packed the auditorium.
