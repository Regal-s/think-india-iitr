§ 01

A “Nation First” attitude — and what it actually means.

Think India is a pan-India movement built around a simple belief: that the best thing a young, talented person can do with their education is turn it outward — toward the nation, toward society, toward problems that actually matter.

On the IIT Roorkee campus, that belief takes the shape of late-night discussions, packed seminar halls, flag-bearing processions down campus roads, and friendships built around ideas bigger than any one person.

We call it a “Nation First” attitude. It doesn't mean blind allegiance to anything. It means asking harder questions, taking longer views, and refusing to let technical expertise live in a bubble.

Our biggest moment each year is the Think India National Convention — a gathering that pulls students in from IITs, NITs, IIMs, IISc, NLUs, and more. The 2024 edition brought over 280 delegates to our campus for three days of honest, sometimes uncomfortable, often inspiring conversation about where India is going — built around the theme of Bharat 4.0, the India of 2047.

But it is not all conferences. Every Independence Day, we spill out onto the campus roads for our Tiranga Yatra. Students, faculty, staff — all together — walking with the flag, feeling a little corny about it and not minding one bit. Those moments matter. They remind us why we are here.

§ 02

Four convictions we keep coming back to.

i.

Nation, but not nationalism.

A “Nation First” lens is not allegiance — it is a habit of asking harder questions. Who does this technology serve? Whose voice is missing from the room? What would a longer view tell us?

ii.

Tradition × Innovation.

Bharat is older than its borders and younger than its possibilities. We celebrate nationalism with a progressive outlook — bridging Indic thought, modern science, and the engineering imagination that IIT Roorkee was built on.

iii.

Thought, then action.

We think, we design, we implement. Strategies that uplift urban and rural Bharat. Initiatives that respond to the environmental, cultural and security needs of our communities — never letting ideas stop at the seminar room.

iv.

A campus, a country, a conversation.

We sustain India-centric dialogue within the campus and across the wider intelligentsia. Chhatra Sansad, lectures, workshops, yatras — different forms of the same insistence that the most important conversations belong in rooms like ours.

§ 03

Selected initiatives from the year.

A sample of what the year has looked like — drawn from ऋतम्, our chapter's first magazine.

Flagship · Convention

Think India National Convention 2024

Three days. Two hundred and eighty delegates from IITs, NITs, IIMs, IISc and NLUs. A single question: what does a self-reliant India look like in 2047?

Themed Bharat 4.0, the 2024 convention turned the IIT Roorkee campus into a working room for the country's next chapter — covering legal reform, media narratives, climate resilience, eastern-Himalayan frontiers, and a student-led Ideathon judged by a panel from NITI Aayog, Cisco Research and i-Hub Gujarat.

Dec 21–23, 2024·280+ delegates
Yatra

Tiranga Yatra

A 600-metre tricolour, 2,000 participants, and the kind of patriotism that doesn't need a stage. Held each Independence Day across the IITR campus.

Aug 15·2024 & 2025 editions
Debate

Chhatra Sansad

A parliamentary-style debate forum — four editions and counting. From the Uniform Civil Code to AI & employment to free welfare schemes, an arena for arguments built and dismantled in public.

2024–2026·4 editions
Lecture

Master Your Mind

A 650-seat audience, a Microsoft engineer who once sat in the same classrooms, and an evening on meditation and the Sanatan way of living. Ancient wisdom made urgently contemporary.

Jul 30, 2025·MAC Auditorium
Swadeshi

Maati-NITI & Khadi Mela

Pottery wheels, terracotta cookware, Madhubani sarees, Bagaru prints, Kashmiri suits. Two melas, nearly 10,000 visitors, and a direct line between artisans and the campus community.

Oct 2025·UG Floor & PIC Ground
Service

Parakram Diwas — Blood Donation Camp

On Netaji's birth anniversary, 400 donors turned up through pouring rain. Courage, compassion, and a quiet refusal to let the weather decide the day.

Jan 23, 2026·SAC, IIT Roorkee
Workshop

Nitishala — Policy Negotiation

A two-hour mock negotiation on siting a new port — environmental compliance, employment, labour law, national development — led by Karmaveer Chakra awardee Mr. Nikhil Arya.

Feb 22, 2025·Rashtram School collab

Voter Awareness — “First Vote, Must Vote.”
